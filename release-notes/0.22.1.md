# OZ Land Claim 0.22.1

- Time-based claim waits now stop at a configurable minimum and maximum, including when expanding a zone. With the default 10-minute base time, the bounds are 1 and 25 minutes per chunk.
- Configure `claimTimeMinFactor` and `claimTimeMaxFactor` to adjust these bounds independently of extra claims earned from playtime.

Existing claims and world data require no migration. Requires OZ Tools 0.27.0 or newer.
